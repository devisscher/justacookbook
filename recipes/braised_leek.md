# Oven-Baked Leeks with Miso Chili Cream

<RecipeImage />

*Serves 4 as a side dish or 2 as a main*

## Ingredients

- 4 large leeks, white and light green parts only, cut into 2-inch barrel rounds
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons white or red miso paste
- 1-2 tablespoons red bean chilli sauce (hot and sour soup sauce)
- 500ml heavy cream
- 125ml dry white wine
- 1 medium red onion, cut into large chunks
- 2 tablespoons fresh tarragon, chopped (or 1 tablespoon dried)
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil (for greasing)
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven**: Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease a baking dish with oil.

2. **Prepare the vegetables**: Cut leeks into 1.5-inch thick barrel-shaped rounds. Cut red onion into large chunks. Rinse leeks thoroughly between layers to remove any dirt. Pat dry and arrange both leeks and onion chunks in the prepared baking dish in a single layer.

3. **Make the sauce**: In a medium saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat.

4. **Add wine and seasonings**: Pour in the white wine and let it bubble for 2-3 minutes to cook off the alcohol. In a small bowl, whisk together the miso paste and chilli sauce until smooth, then add to the pan with the wine.

5. **Finish the sauce**: Slowly stir in the cream and bring to a gentle simmer. Cook for 2-3 minutes, then season with salt and pepper to taste.

6. **Bake**: Pour the hot cream sauce over the leek barrels and onion chunks in the baking dish. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes until leeks are tender when pierced with a knife.

7. **Broil to finish**: Remove the foil and turn oven to broil (or increase to 220°C/425°F). Broil for 10 minutes until the top is golden brown and bubbling.

8. **Garnish and serve**: Sprinkle with fresh tarragon just before serving.

## Serving Suggestions

- Excellent alongside grilled fish, chicken, or pork
- Can be served as a vegetarian main with a poached egg on top
- Pairs well with roasted root vegetables

## Tips

- Clean leeks thoroughly as they can be quite sandy
- The miso adds deep umami while the chilli sauce provides heat and tang
- Can be made ahead and reheated gently
