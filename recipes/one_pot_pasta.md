# One Pot Pasta

<RecipeImage />

*Serves 2-3 people*

A simple and delicious pasta dish that comes together in one pot for easy cleanup. This recipe combines tender vegetables, earthy mushrooms, and fresh spinach in a silky butter sauce, all finished with Parmesan cheese.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup carrot, diced
- 1/2 cup red onion, diced
- 1/2 cup celery, diced
- 2 tablespoons butter (for sautéing)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 250g pasta shells
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
- 1 cup fresh spinach
- 1/2 cup cold butter
- 1-2 teaspoons vinegar (white wine or apple cider)
- Parmesan cheese, grated (for serving)
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. **Sauté the vegetables**: In a large, deep skillet or pot, heat the olive oil and 2 tablespoons of butter over medium heat. Add the diced carrots, red onion, and celery. Sauté for 5 minutes until vegetables are softened and fragrant.

2. **Toast the pasta**: Add the dry pasta to the pan with the vegetables. Stir frequently for 2-3 minutes, allowing the pasta edges to brown slightly. This adds a nutty flavor to the dish.

3. **Add broth and cook**: Pour in the chicken broth and bring to a simmer. Cook the pasta, stirring occasionally, allowing the liquid to reduce. Add water as needed (about 1/4 cup at a time) until the pasta is just under al dente.

4. **Add mushrooms**: Stir in the sliced mushrooms and cook for 3-4 minutes until they soften and release their moisture.

5. **Add spinach**: Add the fresh spinach and stir until wilted, about 1-2 minutes.

6. **Finish with butter**: Remove from heat and stir in the cold butter, allowing it to melt and create a silky sauce. Add the vinegar and season with salt and pepper to taste.

7. **Serve**: Divide into bowls and top generously with freshly grated Parmesan cheese.

## Tips

- The key is to add liquid gradually - you want the pasta to absorb the flavors while cooking
- Cold butter added at the end creates a restaurant-quality creamy finish
- Feel free to substitute vegetable broth for a vegetarian version
- Add a pinch of red pepper flakes for some heat
