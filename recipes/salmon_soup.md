# Lohikeitto (Finnish Salmon Soup)

<RecipeImage />

*Serves 4-6 people*

A comforting Finnish classic featuring tender salmon, potatoes, and fresh dill in a creamy broth. This hearty soup is perfect for cold days and tastes even better the next day.

## Ingredients

- 500g fresh salmon fillet, skin removed, cut into chunks
- 600g baby potatoes, halved or quartered
- 1 large leek, white and light green parts only, sliced
- 2-3 medium carrots, sliced diagonally 
- 200ml heavy cream (or 100ml cream + 100ml milk)
- 1-2 fish stock cubes or 1L fish stock
- Fresh dill, chopped (about 2-3 tablespoons)
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 2 tablespoons butter
- Salt and white pepper to taste
- Bay leaves (optional, 2-3 leaves)

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the base**: In a large pot, melt butter over medium heat. Add the sliced leek and sauté for 3-4 minutes until softened.

2. **Add vegetables**: Add the potatoes and carrots to the pot. Pour in enough water to cover the vegetables (about 1L). Add the fish stock cube(s) or use prepared fish stock. Bring to a boil.

3. **Cook vegetables**: Reduce heat and simmer for 15-20 minutes until potatoes are tender when pierced with a fork.

4. **Add salmon**: Gently add the salmon chunks to the pot. Simmer for 5-7 minutes until the salmon is just cooked through and flakes easily.

5. **Finish the soup**: Reduce heat to low. Slowly stir in the cream to prevent curdling. Add chopped dill and lemon juice. Season with salt and white pepper to taste.

6. **Serve**: Ladle into bowls and garnish with extra fresh dill. Serve with dark rye bread and butter.

## Tips

- Don't overcook the salmon - it should be tender and flaky
- For a lighter version, use milk instead of cream
- Some Finns add a splash of white wine for extra flavor
- The soup tastes even better the next day!
