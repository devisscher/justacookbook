### [Oven-Baked Leeks with Miso Chili Cream](./braised_leek)
*Serves 4 as a side dish or 2 as a main*

A rich and savory dish combining the mild sweetness of leeks with the umami depth of miso and a spicy kick from chili sauce. Perfect as a side dish or vegetarian main course.

### [Lohikeitto (Finnish Salmon Soup)](./salmon_soup)
*Serves 4-6 people*

A comforting Finnish classic featuring tender salmon, potatoes, and fresh dill in a creamy broth. This hearty soup is perfect for cold days and tastes even better the next day.
